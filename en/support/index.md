# ACNH Notes by Modong Support

[한국어](/modongnote-site/support/)

You can send a question, issue report, or suggestion from inside ACNH Notes by Modong after signing in:

**My > Help & feedback**

You may add a reply email. If you leave it blank, the app uses the email associated with your signed-in account. The app shows the inquiry status, and replies that need a response are sent by email.

If you cannot sign in, or if your request concerns your account or personal information, email:

**modongnote@gmail.com**

## Helpful details to include

- Device type, such as iPhone or Android
- Operating-system version
- App version
- The screen where the issue occurred
- Steps that reproduce the issue
- A screenshot, if available

Do not include passwords, verification codes, access tokens, or other secrets.

## Account and privacy requests

You can request access, correction, or deletion of account information at the same email address.

To delete your account in the app:

**My > scroll to the bottom > Delete account**

- [Account deletion instructions](/modongnote-site/en/account-deletion/)
